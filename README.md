# people_analytics_&_ai_transformation_portfolio

Welcome to my portfolio, showcasing practical applications of People Analytics, workforce intelligence and AI-enabled transformation.

This portfolio demonstrates how data, technology and responsible AI can support better workforce decisions, improve organisational effectiveness and enable scalable business growth.

## Portfolio Focus

- **People Analytics:** Workforce trends, headcount analysis, recruitment metrics, skills intelligence and workforce costs.
- **Strategic Workforce Planning:** Headcount forecasting, scenario modelling, recruitment capacity and organisational planning.
- **AI Transformation:** Identifying opportunities to apply AI, redesigning processes and developing practical AI-enabled workflows.
- **Responsible AI:** Data privacy, governance, fairness, human oversight and responsible implementation.
